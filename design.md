# Design — CoreD

A practical B2B site for owners and operators comparing AI, automation and security work. The marketing pages use CoreD’s pre-redesign layout; the journal retains the newer editorial design.

- Marketing: black Home hero with a centered armored robot background, orange AI chip and blue security shield, plus clear routes into AI and cybersecurity. Cream sections with rounded cards and project galleries follow the original case-study layout.
- Content: editorial journal index with a featured guide and topic links; narrow Inter reading column for full articles and clear headings for legal pages.
- Navigation: the original floating pill on marketing pages, with one breakpoint for the menu and CTA. The journal shares the same masthead and footer.
- Palette: warm paper, charcoal ink, amber accent; the Home hero is always black by brief. Tokens live in [tokens.css](tokens.css).
- Typography: Inter Tight for upright display, Inter for controls and general body text, Inter for article prose.
- Motion: section and staggered heading reveals. The hero background stays still so its face and emblems remain easy to read. Content is visible before animation and reduced-motion settings stop movement.
- Proof: real CoreD and client assets. Distinguish screenshots and documented functionality from adoption, metrics and production status.
- References: [Eloqwnt](https://www.eloqwnt.com/) for service/work pacing and large project proof; [Medium](https://medium.com/) for editorial browsing and article measure. [CoreDhristi](https://www.coredhristi.com/) is the related marketplace and has its own account and purchasing flow.
